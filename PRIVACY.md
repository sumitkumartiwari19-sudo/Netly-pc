# Netly privacy

Last updated: November 2026

**Short version:** Netly has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. It does not send your data to the developer.

## What stays on your PC
- Settings, the downloads list, the library list and picture previews are stored in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Netly` and in the download folder you choose.
- If you choose a cookies file or a browser for sign-in (Settings → YouTube access), it is passed to the bundled yt-dlp only to talk to YouTube. Netly does not copy, upload or log cookies.

## Internet connections Netly makes
- **YouTube / Google video servers**: to search, play and download what you ask for.
- **GitHub**: to update yt-dlp (if automatic engine update is on) and to check whether a new Netly release exists (can be turned off in Settings). These are public requests with no personal data.
- **Image servers (i.ytimg.com)**: video thumbnails.

Your IP address is visible to these services, as with any app or browser. A proxy can be set in Settings.

## Feedback
If you use "Send feedback by email" or "Report a problem", the message is created in your mail app or on GitHub and sent only when you send it. The email includes only the Netly version and Windows version.
